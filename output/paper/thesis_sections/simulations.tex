\section{Simulations}
The following parameters were chosen and 1700 samples generated:
$$\phi = 0.5 \qquad \theta = 0.1 \qquad \sigma_\eta^2 = 1 \qquad \sigma_\varepsilon^2 = 1 \qquad T = 10,000,000 \qquad n = 2$$

To evaluate whether the sample covariance matrix of parameter estimates differs significantly from the predicted asymptotic variance, Box-M tests were conducted. By virtue of certain parameters being functions of others, the theorized covariance matrix is singular, making the test impossible. However, the submatrices corresponding to $(\phi, \theta_b, \sigma_b^2)$ and $(\phi, \theta, \sigma_\eta^2)$ both allow for the test to proceed, and both tests yielded insignificant p-values, supporting the validity of this method.
