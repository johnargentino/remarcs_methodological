% \section{Analyzing ARMA-Noise Processes}

Remaining in our best-case scenario of perfectly estimating $\beta$, our objective is to fit estimates for $\phi,\theta,\sigma_\eta^2,\sigma_\varepsilon^2$ with observations $\{w_{ti}\}$, the time-step averages of which form a time series $\bar{\mathbf{w}}$ that is parameterized by $\phi, \theta_b, \sigma_b^2$ (with innovations $\{b_t\})$. ARMA-Noise models are fit with a method detailed by Wong and Miller (1990) \cite{WongMiller1990}.
