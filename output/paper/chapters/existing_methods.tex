\section{Repeated Cross Sectional Data}

Having detailed some useful machinery for analyzing univariate time series, we now consider data obtained through multiple observations, each with i.i.d noise, made at each time step. Such data are defined as repeated cross-sections (RCS), and they have long been the subject of analysis. When cross-sections are taken with high frequency, the possibility of temporal correlation arises. While panel data consist of a single sample being observed repeatedly, RCS data do not necessarily have any repeated observations, ruling out the treatment of the data as a multivariate time series. It is often instead the population mean that is treated as a time series, with individual observations modeled as a realization of that mean with some added noise.
