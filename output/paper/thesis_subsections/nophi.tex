\subsection{$\phi = 0, \theta \neq 0$}

Let's begin with the case that the 

$w_t = (1 + \theta_b)b_t, \qquad b_t \sim N(0,\sigma_b^2)$

$$\sqrt{T}\left[\left(\begin{array}{c}
     \hat{\theta}_b \\
      \hat{\sigma}_b^2 \\
      \hat{\sigma}_a^2
\end{array}\right) - \left((\begin{array}{c}
    {\theta}_b \\
      {\sigma}_b^2 \\
     {\sigma}_a^2
\end{array}\right) \right] -> N(0,\Sigma_o),\qquad \Sigma_o = \left[\begin{array}{ccc}
    (1-\theta_b^2) & 0 & 0 \\
    0 & 2\sigma_b^2 & 0 \\
    0 & 0 & 2\sigma_a^2(n-1)^{-1}
\end{array}\right]$$

$$ (1 + \theta_bB)b_t = (1 + \theta B) \eta_t + a_t $$

$$\sigma_{a*}^2 - \sigma_a^2 = 0$$

$$ (1 + \theta_b^2)\sigma_b^2 - (1 + \theta^2)\sigma_\eta^2 - \sigma_a^2 = 0$$

$$ \theta_b\sigma_b^2 - \theta\sigma_\eta^2 = 0 $$

$$ \frac{\partial H}{\partial \xi_1} = \left[\begin{array}{ccc}
    1 & 0 & 0 \\
    0 & -2\theta\sigma_\eta^2 & -(1 + \theta^2) \\
    0 & -\sigma_\eta^2 & -\theta
\end{array}\right] $$

$$ \frac{\partial H}{\partial \xi_0} = \left[\begin{array}{ccc}
    0 & 0 & -1 \\
    2\theta_b\sigma_b^2 & (1 + \theta_b^2) & -1 \\
    \sigma_b^2 & \theta_b & 0
\end{array}\right] $$

To inform how inference informs model selection in this case, let's explore how the inclusion of an MA term interacts with the estimation of $\phi$ and $\sigma_\eta^2$ in the event that the true MA term $\theta = 0$.
