\subsection{$\phi \neq 0, \theta = 0$}
Let's begin our cases with the same model as the noise component proposed by Modugno et al, namely an AR(1) process with added individual noise.
% Even if $\uu$ is strictly an AR(1) process, adding noise produces an ARMA(1,1) process. 


$$ w_t = u_t + a_t $$
Now knowing that $\mathbf{w}$ is an ARMA(1,1) process with MA parameter $\theta_b$ and innovation variance $\sigma_b^2$, we recall Equation \ref{eq:B_w=arplusnoise}:

$$ (1 - \phi B)^{-1}(1 + \theta_bB)b_t = (1 - \phi B)^{-1} \eta_t + a_t $$

$$ (1 + \theta_bB)b_t = \eta_t + (1 - \phi B)a_t $$

$$ (1 + \theta_b^2)\sigma_b^2 = \sigma_\eta^2 + (1 + \phi^2)\sigma_a^2 $$

$$ {\sigma}_\eta^2 = (1 + {\theta}_b^2){\sigma}_b^2 - (1 + {\phi}^2){\sigma}_a^2 = H $$

$$ \hat{\sigma}_\eta^2 = (1 + \hat{\theta}_b^2)\hat{\sigma}_b^2 - (1 + \hat{\phi}^2)\hat{\sigma}_a^2 $$

$$ \frac{\partial H_\eta}{\partial \phi} = -2\phi\sigma_a^2$$

$$ \frac{\partial H_\eta}{\partial \theta_b} = 2\theta_b\sigma_b^2$$

$$ \frac{\partial H_\eta}{\partial \sigma_b^2} = 1 + \theta_b^2$$

$$ \frac{\partial H_\eta}{\partial \sigma_a^2} = -(1 + \phi^2)$$
