    \sigma^2(\phi - \phi^3 + \theta - \phi^2\theta + \phi(\phi + \theta)^2) = \sigma_\eta^2\phi
\end{cases} $$

After expanding we get:

$$\begin{cases}
    \sigma^2(1 - \phi ^ 2 + (\phi^2 + 2\phi\theta + \theta^2)) =  \sigma_\eta^2 + \sigma_a^2(1 - \phi ^ 2) \\
    \sigma^2(\phi - \phi^3 + \theta - \phi^2\theta + (\phi^3 + 2\phi^2\theta + \phi\theta^2)) = \sigma_\eta^2\phi
\end{cases} $$

The expressions simplify after combining like terms:

$$\begin{cases}
    \sigma^2(1  + 2\phi\theta + \theta^2) =  \sigma_\eta^2 + \sigma_a^2(1 - \phi ^ 2) \\
    \sigma^2(\phi  + \theta + \phi^2\theta + \phi\theta^2) = \sigma_\eta^2\phi
\end{cases} $$

Setting the second equation equal to $\sigma^2$ and substituting into the first yields:

$$\begin{cases}
    \sigma_\eta^2\phi   (1  + 2\phi\theta + \theta^2) =  \sigma_\eta^2(\phi  + \theta + \phi^2\theta + \phi\theta^2) + \sigma_a^2(1 - \phi ^ 2)(\phi  + \theta + \phi^2\theta + \phi\theta^2) \\
    \sigma^2 = \sigma_\eta^2\phi/(\phi  + \theta + \phi^2\theta + \phi\theta^2) 
\end{cases} $$

The first equation can now be rearranged into one that's quadratic for $\theta$:
$$ \theta^2(\sigma_\eta^2\phi - \sigma_\eta^2\phi - \sigma_a^2(1-\phi^2)\phi) + \theta(2\sigma_\eta^2\phi^2 - \sigma_\eta^2(1 + \phi^2) - \sigma_a^2(1-\phi^2)(1 +\phi^2)) + (\sigma_\eta^2\phi - \sigma_\eta^2\phi - \sigma_a^2\phi(1-\phi^2))
= 0 $$

Eliminating terms that cancel and negating the the whole equation produces:

$$ \theta^2 \underset{a}{\underbrace{( \sigma_a^2(1-\phi^2)\phi)}} + \theta \underset{b}{\underbrace{ (\sigma_\eta^2(1-\phi^2) + \sigma_a^2(1-\phi^4))}} + \underset{c}{\underbrace{( \sigma_a^2\phi(1-\phi^2))}}
